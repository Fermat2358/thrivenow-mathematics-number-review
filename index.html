KS3 Foundations in Number

Mastery-style sessions for building secure, connected number sense

Welcome to KS3 Foundations in Number.

This collection contains 19 interactive ThriveNow Mathematics sessions designed to strengthen confidence, fluency and reasoning with number. Learners can follow the suggested pathway or begin with the area that best matches what they are ready to explore.

Purpose: a flexible KS3 foundations pathway organised by mathematical readiness rather than year-group label.

Seeds of Change: National Curriculum outcomes guide fluency, reasoning, problem-solving and connected mathematical understanding.

Session design: each session follows the Verse-al Five Ways of Knowing and uses focused accordion sections so learners can explore one mathematical perspective at a time.

Choose your starting point

Start at the beginning or open the area that feels most useful right now.

Explore in manageable sections

Each session opens one main perspective at a time to reduce unnecessary scrolling and overload.

Practise and apply

Practice Companions build challenge progressively, followed by a Final Task that brings the mathematics together.
